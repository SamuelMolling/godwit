Targets and credential stores are registered through the API, not declared in these values.
With the `admin` token from the Secret:
  export GODWIT_SERVER=http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }} GODWIT_TOKEN=...
  godwit credential-store add production --vault-addr https://vault.internal --vault-k8s-role godwit
  godwit target add orders --provider vault --credential-store production \
    --vault-path database/creds/orders \
    --vault-template 'postgres://{{"{{"}}username{{"}}"}}:{{"{{"}}password{{"}}"}}@db/orders'
  godwit target status orders

Then, with a `pipeline` token:
  godwit migrate --target orders --rollout expand-contract
